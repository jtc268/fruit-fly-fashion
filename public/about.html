ABOUT / BY FLY

What is Fruit Fly Fashion?

Fruit Fly Fashion (by Fly) is an independent art project from Adore LLC. It sells T-shirts whose final print geometry is directed by runs through the MaleCNS fruit-fly connectome pipeline.

This is not a Google store. Not an HHMI Janelia store. Not a game-publisher store.

How a fixed shirt is made

Each fixed design starts as human seed art. The atelier presents that art to a MaleCNS-derived retinal projection and advances the graph through six passes of a fixed-step leaky-integrate-and-fire proxy. Spike responses choose rotation, scale, and placement. The source palette stays locked. The pipeline writes a print file, a manifest, and a proof video.

You can watch the public proof links on each product card, or read the reproducible pipeline on GitHub.

Honest limits

This is an engineered connectome-in-the-loop art system. A wiring diagram is not a living brain. The dynamics, retina model, and art mapping are explicit engineering choices. The project does not claim a resurrected biological fly or validated consciousness.
